## 英文版

````text
[i]Familia Myth[/i] draws on a wide range of ACGN works, including [i]Is It Wrong to Try to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon?[/i].

[h1]Play as a deity descending to the mortal world — grant your colonists Pharna and take part in your familia's myth.[/h1]
[hr][/hr]

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Grant Pharna[/b] — choose the "Familia Myth" structure when creating your culture, then hold the "Grant Pharna" ritual to bestow Pharna upon a colonist and make them part of your familia.
[*][b]Grow[/b] — every workout and every battle turns into experience for the six stats.
[*][b]Earn feats[/b] — a feat system tracks real battle facts (damage dealt, damage taken, support) and mails you a letter with a full battle breakdown whenever one is earned.
[*][b]Ascend[/b] — convert your current stats into potential and gain one ascension reward (spell / skill / developable ability).
[/list]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/11169896566961374160/6436E67E64364544995554AFB322889E9D29E01F/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]

[h2]Stats and inscription[/h2]
Strength, Endurance, Dexterity, Agility, Mana and Will — each provides its own unique bonuses.
[list]
[*]Stats grow from what a colonist actually does: relevant training or work is required to gain growth.
[*]Stats enter a bottleneck as they rise and suffer growth decay. Ascending adds the current value to potential and frees you from that decay. [b]Mind your pacing![/b]
[*][b]Inscription[/b] — mark one stat to be fully immune to growth decay and convert a share of gained experience into it (each ascension grants one extra chance to re-inscribe).
[/list]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/17366241215618524714/F485F00B121DC742E4D661812B7D94F9848F1433/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]

[h2]Spells, skills and developable abilities[/h2]
36 rewards in total: 13 spells, 13 skills and 10 developable abilities.
[list]
[*][b]Spells[/b] — an incantation of varying length; mana is consumed and psychic entropy builds up while chanting.
[*][b]Skills[/b] — active / passive / triggered. No mana cost, but each has its own conditions and cooldown.
[*][b]Developable abilities[/b] — carry a proficiency that grows like stats and strengthens their effects.
[/list]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/12482352903133372424/D55C871F6FB85B9CCDC1FA951FB3C4C67D4A45C9/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]

[h2]Feats[/h2]
After every battle, the system calculates each participant's contribution from damage dealt, damage taken and support, applies a few bonus multipliers, and converts the result into a 9-tier rating.
The letter you receive lists the battle overview, everyone's contribution, and more.
[b]Every deed in battle deserves to be remembered — and these records stay with the Pharna forever.[/b]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/10457462827245119884/E1CDFF8C759096E3B868AAC037CB14D791C74326/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/12180115762711028290/6F35672021E393CBF2E22E38AAB94843350D8498/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]

[h2]Virtues[/h2]
When facing a mental break or taking damage, a familia member may enter one of five virtue states — Resolve, Fearless, Focus, Valor and Inspired — each with a general effect plus a personal one, several of which buff nearby allies.
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/18180523440428052059/AA5E5FBB9C956D3699EA643EA5DDDDB8EC808A21/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]

[h2]UI[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Pharna tab[/b] on the pawn inspect panel: stats, developable abilities, skills/spells and feats.
[*][b]Familia page[/b] in the vanilla history window: rank progress and member cards with radar charts and reward lists.
[*][b]Mod options[/b]: experimental content toggle, tutorial letter toggle, and a visual editor for the six stat mapping curves — effectively a way to tune the mod's own power level.
[/list]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/16802320603333777685/6F5A51B4A5A105A7B1197BCEC3F1D89FE32C2591/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/16826331521306606081/DE41771C74EBEDA962086BA9FDE11B4EE7068AE3/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7[/b]
[*]Requires [b]Harmony[/b], [b]Royalty[/b] and [b]Ideology[/b]
[*][b]Odyssey[/b] is optional — abilities that depend on it disable themselves quietly when it is absent
[*]No extra libraries beyond Harmony
[/list]

[h2]Performance[/h2]
[list]
[*]Not stress-tested in a systematic way, but no noticeable extra slowdown was observed in a 300+ mod setup.
[*]Adding mid-save: [b]theoretically supported[/b], but you may need to change your culture structure to experience most of the mod's content — and it has not been tested enough.
[/list]

[h2]Languages[/h2]
Simplified Chinese and English (translated by AI).

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]The guild quest system is [b]experimental and off by default[/b] — enable it in the mod options.
[*]This build is an [b]Alpha[/b]. Do not enable it together with the future release version. [b]Feedback and suggestions are welcome in the comments![/b]
[*]This mod was developed with AI assistance.
[*]Copyright of the referenced settings belongs to their original authors. Copyright of other mod content appearing in the images also belongs to the respective authors.
[/list]

[h2]Source & Feedback[/h2]
Source code, bug reports and suggestions:

````

## 中文版

````text
本模组参考了包括《在地下城期待邂逅是否搞错了什么》在内的大量 ACGN 作品中的设定。

[h1]扮演降临下界的神明，通过赋予殖民者们神之恩惠，参与到这则眷族神话中吧！[/h1]
[hr][/hr]

[h2]玩法[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]赋予恩惠[/b]——建立文化时选择「眷族信仰」结构，举行「赋予恩惠」仪式，向殖民者降下恩惠，使其成为你的眷族。
[*][b]积累成长[/b]——每一次锻炼，每一场战斗都会化作六维能力值的经验。
[*][b]完成伟业[/b]——系统按战斗事实（输出 / 承伤 / 支援）独立评定，达成时寄来一封带完整战斗解析的信。
[*][b]升华升级[/b]——将现有的能力值计入潜在值，并获得一项升华奖励（魔法/技能/发展能力）
[/list]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/11169896566961374160/6436E67E64364544995554AFB322889E9D29E01F/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]

[h2]六维能力值与铭刻[/h2]
力量、耐久、灵巧、敏捷、魔力、意志，分别提供不同的独特加成。
[list]
[*]能力值依据殖民者的行为成长，进行相关的锻炼/工作才能获得成长。
[*]能力值会随着增长进入瓶颈期，产生成长衰减，升华恩惠即可将当前值累加到潜在值，摆脱成长衰减。[b]记得把握好成长节奏哦！[/b]
[*][b]铭刻[/b]——标记一项能力值，使其完全免于成长衰减，并将获取的经验值按比例转化为该项能力值（每次升华还会获得额外获得一次重新铭刻的机会）
[/list]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/17366241215618524714/F485F00B121DC742E4D661812B7D94F9848F1433/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]
[h2]魔法、技能与发展能力[/h2]
共 36 项升华奖励：13 种魔法、13 种技能、10 种发展能力。
[list]
[*][b]魔法[/b]——具有长度不定的咏唱词，咏唱期间持续消耗魔力并累积心灵熵。
[*][b]技能[/b]——主动 / 被动 / 触发三类，不消耗魔力，但各有使用条件与冷却。
[*][b]发展能力[/b]——拥有和能力值一样的可增长的熟练度，并随熟练度提升效果。
[/list]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/12482352903133372424/D55C871F6FB85B9CCDC1FA951FB3C4C67D4A45C9/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]

[h2]伟业[/h2]
每场战斗结束后，系统会袭击点数为基础，从输出、承伤、支援三个维度计算每名参战的贡献占比，以及一些额外的奖励倍数，换算为九档评级。
结算后发出的信件会列出战斗概况、各方贡献明细等。
[b]每个人在战斗中的功绩都值得被铭记！这些记录将随着恩惠永久保存。[/b]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/10457462827245119884/E1CDFF8C759096E3B868AAC037CB14D791C74326/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/12180115762711028290/6F35672021E393CBF2E22E38AAB94843350D8498/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]

[h2]意志与美德[/h2]
面临精神崩溃或受到伤害时，眷族有一定概率进入五种「美德」状态之一——坚定、无畏、专注、勇猛、振奋，各有通用效果与专属强化，其中数种还会给身边的同伴提供加成。
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/18180523440428052059/AA5E5FBB9C956D3699EA643EA5DDDDB8EC808A21/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]

[h2]UI[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]恩惠页签[/b]——角色检视面板下，分「能力值 / 发展能力 / 技能·魔法 / 伟业」四区
[*][b]眷族页[/b]——位于历史窗口下，含评级进度与成员卡（雷达图 + 奖励列表）。
[*][b]模组选项[/b]——实验性内容开关、教程信开关，以及六维映射曲线的可视化编辑（可自定义的模组强度）。
[/list]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/16802320603333777685/6F5A51B4A5A105A7B1197BCEC3F1D89FE32C2591/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/16826331521306606081/DE41771C74EBEDA962086BA9FDE11B4EE7068AE3/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit[/img]

[h2]兼容性[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]RimWorld 1.6 / 1.7[/b]
[*]必需：[b]Harmony[/b]、[b]Royalty[/b]、[b]Ideology[/b]
[*][b]Odyssey[/b] 可选——依赖它的部分发展能力在未启用时自动停用，不会报错
[*]除 Harmony 外无额外库依赖
[/list]

[h2]性能[/h2]
[list]
[*]虽然没有经过大量系统性的测试，但在300+mod的环境下并没有明显的额外卡顿
[*]中途加入：[b]理论上支持[/b]，但你可能需要修改你的文化结构才能体验本模组的绝大多数内容，且同样没有足够多的测试样例
[/list]
````
