## 英文版

````text
[i]Familia Myth[/i] draws on a wide range of ACGN works, including [i]Is It Wrong to Try to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon?[/i].
[h1]Play as a deity descending to the mortal world — grant your colonists Pharna and take part in your familia's myth.[/h1]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Grant Pharna[/b] — pick the "Familia Myth" structure when creating your culture, then hold the "Grant Pharna" ritual to turn a colonist into a member of your familia.
[*][b]Grow[/b] — every workout and every battle turns into experience for the six stats.
[*][b]Earn feats[/b] — a feat system tracks real battle facts (damage dealt, damage taken, support) and mails you a letter with a full battle breakdown whenever one is earned.
[*][b]Ascend[/b] — convert your current stats into potential, then pick 1 of 3 ascension rewards.
[/list]

[h2]Stats and inscription[/h2]
Strength, Endurance, Dexterity, Agility, Mana and Will — each provides its own unique bonuses.
[list]
[*]Stats grow from what a colonist actually does: you have to train or work in the relevant activity to gain growth.
[*][b]Inscription[/b] — mark one stat to be immune to growth decay and receive a share of daily experience. Each ascension restores one inscription.
[/list]

[h2]Spells, skills and developable abilities[/h2]
36 rewards in total: 13 spells, 13 skills and 10 developable abilities.
[list]
[*][b]Spells[/b] need an incantation: the chant appears line by line, consuming mana and building psychic entropy.
[*][b]Skills[/b] come in active / passive / triggered flavours. No mana cost, but each has its own conditions and cooldown.
[*][b]Developable abilities[/b] carry a proficiency that grows like stats, with stronger effects as it rises.
[/list]

[h2]Feats[/h2]
After a battle ends, each familia member is scored from damage dealt, damage taken and support, then multiplied by battle-wide and personal traits (against the odds, flawless victory, crushing victory, lone defender) into a 9-tier result.
The letter you receive shows the battle overview, everyone's contribution, the trait multipliers, and whether the next ascension requirement is already met.

[h2]Virtues[/h2]
When facing a mental break or taking damage, a familia member may enter one of five virtue states — Resolve, Fearless, Focus, Valor and Inspired — each with a general effect plus a personal one, several of which buff nearby allies.

[h2]UI[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Pharna tab[/b] on the pawn inspect panel: stats, developable abilities, skills/spells and feats.
[*][b]Familia page[/b] in the vanilla history window: rank progress and member cards with radar charts and reward lists.
[*][b]Mod options[/b]: experimental content toggle, tutorial letter toggle, and a visual editor for the six stat mapping curves — effectively a way to tune the mod's own power level.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7[/b]
[*]Requires [b]Harmony[/b], [b]Royalty[/b] and [b]Ideology[/b]
[*][b]Odyssey[/b] is optional — abilities that depend on it disable themselves quietly when it is absent
[*]No extra libraries beyond Harmony
[/list]

[h2]Languages[/h2]
Simplified Chinese and English, key-for-key symmetric.

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]The guild quest system is [b]experimental and off by default[/b] — enable it in the mod options.
[/list]

[h2]Source & Feedback[/h2]
Source code, bug reports and suggestions:
[url=https://github.com/yanyu-TAT/Rimworld-Familia_Myth]GitHub — Rimworld-Familia_Myth[/url]
````

## 中文版

````text
《在边缘世界寻求邂逅是否搞错了什么？》是一个参考了包括《在地下城期待邂逅是否搞错了什么》在内的大量 ACGN 作品的 RimWorld 模组。
[h1]扮演降临下界的神明，通过赋予殖民者们神之恩惠，参与到这则眷族神话中吧！[/h1]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]玩法[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]赋予恩惠[/b]——建立文化时选用「眷族信仰」结构，再举行「赋予恩惠」仪式，让一名殖民者成为眷族成员。
[*][b]积累成长[/b]——每一次锻炼，每一场战斗都会化作六维能力值的经验。
[*][b]完成伟业[/b]——系统按战斗事实（输出 / 承伤 / 支援）独立评定，达成时寄来一封带完整战斗解析的信。
[*][b]升华升级[/b]——将现有的能力值计入潜在值，并从三项升华奖励中择一获得。
[/list]

[h2]六维能力值与铭刻[/h2]
力量、耐久、灵巧、敏捷、魔力、意志，分别提供不同的独特加成。
[list]
[*]能力值依据殖民者的行为成长，进行相关的锻炼/工作才能获得成长。
[*][b]铭刻[/b]——标记一项能力值，使其完全免于成长衰减，并把日常经验按比例转给它；每次升华恢复一次铭刻机会。
[/list]

[h2]魔法、技能与发展能力[/h2]
共 36 项升华奖励：13 种魔法、13 种技能、10 种发展能力。
[list]
[*][b]魔法[/b]需要咏唱——咏唱词逐句浮现，期间持续消耗魔力并累积心灵熵。
[*][b]技能[/b]分主动 / 被动 / 触发三类，不消耗魔力，但各有使用条件与冷却。
[*][b]发展能力[/b]和能力值一样的可增长的熟练度，随熟练度提升效果。
[/list]

[h2]伟业[/h2]
一场战斗结束后，系统以袭击点数为基础，从输出、承伤、支援三个维度计算每名眷族的贡献占比，再叠加战斗层与个人层的性质系数（以寡敌众、无伤克敌、摧枯拉朽、一夫当关），换算为九档得分。
收到的信件会列出战斗概况、各方贡献明细、性质加成，以及「是否已满足下一次升华条件」的结论。

[h2]意志与美德[/h2]
面临精神崩溃或受到伤害时，眷族有一定概率进入五种「美德」状态之一——坚定、无畏、专注、勇猛、振奋，各有通用效果与专属强化，其中数种还会给身边的同伴提供加成。

[h2]UI[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]恩惠页签[/b]——角色检视面板下，分「能力值 / 发展能力 / 技能·魔法 / 伟业」四区
[*][b]眷族页[/b]——位于历史窗口下，含评级进度与成员卡（雷达图 + 奖励列表）。
[*][b]模组选项[/b]——实验性内容开关、教程信开关，以及六维映射曲线的可视化编辑（可自定义的模组强度）。
[/list]

[h2]兼容性[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]RimWorld 1.6 / 1.7[/b]
[*]必需：[b]Harmony[/b]、[b]Royalty[/b]、[b]Ideology[/b]
[*][b]Odyssey[/b] 可选——依赖它的部分发展能力在未启用时自动停用，不会报错
[*]除 Harmony 外无额外库依赖
[/list]

[h2]语言[/h2]
简体中文与 English，中英逐键对称。

[h2]注意[/h2]
[list]
[*]公会任务系统为[b]实验性内容，默认关闭[/b]，入口在模组选项。
[/list]

[h2]源码与反馈[/h2]
源码、问题反馈与建议：
````
